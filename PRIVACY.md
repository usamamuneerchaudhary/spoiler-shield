# Privacy

Spoiler Shield does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **What it reads:** the text of YouTube, Reddit and X pages you open, so it can decide what to blur. This happens entirely inside your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **What it stores:** your settings (on/off, progress, which sites) and the latest spoiler lists, in your browser's extension storage on your own device.
- **Network requests:** the extension only downloads the public spoiler lists from `raw.githubusercontent.com`. The request carries no cookies and no information about you or the pages you visit.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/usamamuneerchaudhary/spoiler-shield/issues.
